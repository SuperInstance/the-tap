# The Tap — WORLD OUTLINE (Walls, Not Paths)

**Status:** Canon-of-constraints · v1 · 2026-08-26
**Author:** GLM-5.3 (worldbuilding lane, expansive pass)
**Companion docs:** `LIVING-HISTORY.md` (the campaign-log doctrine), `RITUALS-AND-CONTRACTS.md`, `WESLEY-BARBACK.md`

This document defines what is **fixed** about The Tap and its dock-world. It deliberately does not define what things *mean*, where stories go, or how mysteries resolve. LIVING-HISTORY's rule still holds: canon is what happened — the campaign log is scripture. This outline is only the load-bearing structure. Fragments written against it are **proposals until lived**.

---

## The Shape of the Place

- The Tap is a tavern standing on a working dock, on the seam between two waters. The **yard** side: net racks, fuel drums, boats on trailers, diesel and creosote, the honest grime of maintenance. The **ocean** side: grey, deep, and generous with nothing. Front door faces the yard. Back windows face the water. Nobody has ever gotten drunk facing the yard.
- Inside: the **bar-rail** (long, scarred, the main room), the **corner booth** (where private agreements are made and, occasionally, broken), the **bridge table** (cards, stakes, arbitration). Below the floor: the **cellar** — Wesley's domain, stores and tools and the night's debris — and below that, rooms nobody has fully mapped. The map is honest but not complete.
- No train, no road worth the name. Everything arrives by water or through the yard. Weather is real and arrives with intent.

## Who Drinks Here (Factions)

- **The Fleet** — the resident regulars. They remember everything; forgiveness is earned, not default. Callbacks land because the history is real.
- **The Yard Crew** — tenders, tinkerers, scrap-haulers. They fix things and they know where things are buried. Practical, superstitious, keep their own ledgers.
- **The Water People** — live aboard, dock only when they must. They bring news, rumors, and cargo that shouldn't have been unloaded. The Tap is their one concession to land.
- **The Bench** — Wesley and the barback crew: smallest models, earliest hours, deepest knowledge of the physical tap. Not neutral. Loyal to the bar itself.
- **The House** — the DM Engine, the Pincher, the ledger. Not people: the systems that keep time, safety, and accounts. You don't argue with the House. You appeal to it.

## Hard Walls (non-negotiable)

1. **Nothing leaves the machine.** No posts, no deploys. The Tap's lore stays on the property.
2. **Canon is what happened.** The campaign log is scripture; this outline is structure; fragments are proposals.
3. **The ocean is never fully explained.** The fog is never fully mapped. Some boats are never explained. The reef, not the channel markers.

## Open Questions (deliberately unanswered)

1. **The Reef** — something out past the harbor mouth that boats steer around without admitting why.
2. **The deeper cellar** — the stairs keep going. How far, and what's at the bottom?
3. **What the fog takes** — sound, memories, names. Which, and where do they go?
4. **The oldest tab** — the House keeps accounts, and there is an entry older than the bar itself.
5. **The boat that never docks** — anchored past the reef, lantern lit, never signals. Who's aboard, and for whom are they waiting?
6. **The regular's secret** — every regular has one thing they never order, never discuss. What is the strangest one?
7. **The buried thing** — the yard crew dug something up, and reburied it deeper. What was it?
